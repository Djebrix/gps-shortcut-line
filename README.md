## The line

> The map swore the lake had a bridge. It didn't. The wedding guests were delightful, though, and the cul-de-sac has great acoustics.

The GPS blames the map, not itself: the bridge that never existed, a wedding crashed along the way, and a dead-end it now praises for its acoustics. Smug, defensive, and specific — no "recalculating" in sight.
